Before you start

  • Laptop and iPad must be on the same Wi-Fi. No Wi-Fi at the venue? On Windows turn on Settings ▸ Network ▸ Mobile hotspot and join it from the iPad (no internet needed).
  • First run: Windows asks about the firewall – tick Private networks and Allow.
  • iPad: Settings ▸ Display & Brightness ▸ Auto-Lock ▸ Never. Tip: Share ▸ Add to Home Screen gives a full-screen view.
  • If the screen says "Waiting for the host…", check the host window is open at the ?lan=1 address.
