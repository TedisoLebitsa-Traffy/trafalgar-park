FLAG IMAGES - CREDITS
=====================
The 195 flag images in this folder (193 UN member states + the 2 UN observer
states, Vatican City and Palestine) were downloaded once with
tools/fetch_flags.py from the free flag service https://flagcdn.com
(a Flagpedia.net service: https://flagpedia.net - "completely free service";
they appreciate a backlink). Flagpedia's flag vectors are derived from
Wikimedia Commons national-flag artwork; national flags are official state
symbols and are commonly used freely for educational / editorial purposes.

The app never contacts flagcdn.com at runtime - everything is served locally
from this folder, so it works fully offline.

To refresh them (for example if a country changes its flag):
    delete the .svg files and run   python tools/fetch_flags.py
